Data Privacy Notice

Republic Act No. 10173: Data Privacy Act of 2012

Pylon Esports, an accredited student organization of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, respects your right to privacy. Before you continue, please read how we handle your personal information.

What we collect: your full name, student number, date of birth, college, program, address, email, and handwritten signature.

Why we collect it: solely to process and keep a record of your Memorandum of Agreement and your membership in Pylon Esports for Academic Year 2026–2027.

How we keep it safe: your information is stored in the organization's private Google account and can only be accessed by authorized officers of Pylon Esports and, when required, its adviser. It will not be sold or shared with third parties, except when required by the University or by law.

How long we keep it: only for as long as your membership agreement is in effect and as required by the University's records policies, after which it will be securely deleted.

Your rights: under RA 10173, you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, and to request the deletion of your personal data. For any concerns, email esports.pylon.manila@gmail.com.

You need to agree to the Data Privacy Notice to sign the MOA. If you have questions, please contact an officer.

IV. STANDARDS OF CONDUCT

  1. General Guidelines: The Member is expected to maintain professionalism. The Organization may discipline any member for actions deemed unacceptable, whether conducting Organization business on or off premises.
  2. Infractions: Unacceptable conduct includes, but is not limited to:
    • Unsatisfactory performance or lack of professionalism.
    • Abusing, threatening violence, or engaging in violence with another member or Organization staff.
    • Fraud, deceit, or stealing/unauthorized possession of property.
    • The use or possession of illegal drugs or weapons.
    • Excessive tardiness, failure to do assigned tasks, or breach of contract.
    • Misusing confidential Organization information.

V. ANTI-HARASSMENT AND DISCRIMINATION

  1. Zero Tolerance: Pylon Esports is committed to a safe, respectful, and lawful community. The Organization does not permit any instance of harassment or discriminatory conduct by or towards anyone.
  2. Protected Classes: Harassment or unlawful discrimination against individuals on the basis of sex, race, color, religion, creed, national origin, sexual orientation, age, pregnancy, identity, ancestry, disability, marital status, or genetic information is strictly prohibited.
  3. Disciplinary Consequence: Any member in violation of this policy will be subject to immediate and severe disciplinary action, including termination.

VI. DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS & IMPEACHMENT

  1. Sanctions: The Organization is liable for its members and has the authority to issue disciplinary actions as they see fit. The magnitude of the disciplinary actions varies and may include a written warning, oral warning, demotion, suspension, or dismissal.
  2. Severe Violations: Disciplinary action may be triggered by having a failure grade, committing heinous acts (e.g., stealing, sexual acts, murder), acts of violence, or pursuing any purpose contrary to ethical principles.
  3. Due Process: All members have the right to due process. Serious misconduct may lead to an investigation and subsequent impeachment upon a 2/3 vote by the Executive Committee.

VII. EFFECTIVITY

This Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing and shall remain in force until the end of the next academic school year, unless the Member graduates during the mid-year or the Agreement is earlier terminated in accordance with the Constitution and By-Laws.
